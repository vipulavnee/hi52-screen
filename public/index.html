Backtest, 210 F&O names, Aug 2025 to Aug 2026 (2,461 signals). Buying a 52-week high and holding 126 days returned +3.30% while Nifty fell 6.18% — +9.47% excess, beating the index 66% of the time. Filtering for volume > 2.5x improved that to +10.40% excess, 67% hit rate, and cut signals from 2,461 to 315 a year. Longer holds beat shorter ones — shares do not decay, so time works for you here rather than against you.

Sizing. Equal rupees, not equal shares. Signal prices span ₹10 to ₹48,895 — a 4,904x spread — so "1 share each" would let whichever dear stock happens to signal dominate the book for reasons unrelated to the signal.

Limits. Prices are Yahoo, verified identical to Kite across 5 stocks and 9 sessions, but the endpoint is undocumented and can break without notice. A split? tag means adjusted and unadjusted closes diverge — a corporate action may distort that stock's 52-week high, so check it by hand. One backtested year is not proof of an edge.
